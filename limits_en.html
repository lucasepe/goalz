Model limits

This tool provides a quick statistical estimate. Please review its limits before using it.

  • It works on single-match predictions using current team parameters.
  • It does not include recent news, injuries, weather, or live odds.
  • Result quality depends on the teams.csv dataset quality.
  • Probabilities are estimates, not guarantees or financial advice.
  • Do not use output as the only betting criterion.
